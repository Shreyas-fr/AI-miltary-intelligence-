FIELD INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY — DOCUMENT B
Classification: UNCLASSIFIED (Test Data Only)
Region of Interest: Syria

1. GROUND FORCE ASSESSMENT
A separate signals-intelligence source places opposing troop strength in the northern
sector closer to 2,500 personnel, roughly double the figure reported in prior assessments.
This estimate has not been reconciled with imagery-based counts.

2. EQUIPMENT INVENTORY
Recent reporting indicates the presence of at least six technical vehicles mounted with
heavy machine guns, along with a small number of mortar systems observed during a
patrol sweep on the western approach road.

3. THREAT ACTIVITY
Twenty-three recorded incidents attributed to this group over the past six months, with
improvised explosive devices as the dominant attack method.

4. CIVILIAN INFRASTRUCTURE
No hospitals, schools, or protected civilian sites are mentioned in this reporting cycle.
